# 🚀 JanHacks 2027

**Kick off 2027 with a two-month teen hackathon (ages 13–18) focused on open-ended creation with zero AI.**

🌐 **Official Website & Sponsorship Info:** https://github.io  
🏆 **Register & Submit on Devpost:** https://janhacks-2027.devpost.com

---

## 👋 Welcome to JanHacks 2027!

JanHacks 2027 offers a two-month window from January 1 to March 1, 2027, for teen students aged 13–18 to build projects without any theme restrictions or AI tools. 

---

## 📅 Timeline & Eligibility

- **Timeline:** Kicks off January 1, 2027 (00:00 UTC), submissions close March 1, 2027 (23:59 UTC), judging runs March 2–7, and winners are announced March 8, 2027.
- **Eligibility:** Open exclusively to students aged 13–18 in middle or high school (or equivalent/homeschooled). No companies, agencies, or adults.
- **Core Rules:** Strictly no AI-generated code or assets. Work solo or in teams of up to 4. Projects must be initiated during the event window.

---

## 📦 How to Submit

Submit via **[Devpost](https://devpost.com)** before March 1, 2027, including:
- Public GitHub/GitLab repository link
- 1–2 minute demo video (5-minute max)
- Project summary and tech stack details

---

All winners receive an official certificate and a spotlight feature:

## 📊 Judging Criteria

Submissions are evaluated on Creativity & Originality, Execution & Polish, Technical Craft & Design, and Fun & Usefulness.
